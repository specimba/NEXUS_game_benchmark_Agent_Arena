How to Play

  • Left-drag — PUSH the fluid away from your focus.
  • Right-drag (or Shift+Left) — PULL the fluid toward your focus.
  • ← / → (or A / D) — tilt gravity to slosh the whole body of liquid.
  • Space — pause · R — restart · M — mute · G — toggle graphics.

Goal: route enough fluid mass into the GOAL zone and hold it there until the hold meter fills. Beat the clock for more stars.
